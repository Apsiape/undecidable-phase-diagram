\section{Tables of the interaction}\label{app:tables}
% =====================================================================

This appendix collects the fields, colours and components of \cref{sec:interaction} in tabular form. Together with the rules (O1)--(P3), (C0)--(C9) and the charge of \cref{sec:interaction}, and the program of \cref{app:machine}, they define $\Phi$. A site value is allowed when it satisfies every rule; neighbouring site values must have equal colours on their common edge.

\paragraph{Size.} The site alphabet is the product of the value sets of \cref{tab:fields}, with $|\Lambda^+_{\mathrm h}|=32|\mathcal S_M|$, $|\Lambda^+_{\mathrm v}|=10368|\mathcal S_M|^2$ and $|\mathcal K|=2^{17}\cdot81\,|\mathcal S_M|(|\mathcal S_M|+1)^2$, where $|\mathcal S_M|=|\Gamma_M|(|Q_M|+1)$, $|\Gamma_M|=96|\Gamma_U|$ and $|Q_M|=54+q_U$, with $q_U$ the number of non-halting states of $U$. It is finite and depends only on $U$. We do not use its size.
